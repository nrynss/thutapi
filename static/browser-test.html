Physical-phone smoke (manual)

Run this page from the same HTTPS deployment on a real phone after the automated contract reports PASS. This checklist records a human observation; it does not claim that a desktop browser or emulator proves iOS audio or keyboard behavior.

  1. From the shelf, tap Make your own book, complete an interview with chips, and confirm the question audio plays. If starting from a cold interview link, tap its speaker control instead.
  2. Focus the text field and confirm it scrolls above the keyboard and the safe-area inset; submit one typed answer.
  3. Finish the interview without a voice sample, then confirm the race advances once for each arriving page event and never before one arrives.

Record the phone model, browser/version, deployment SHA, date, and pass/fail result in the T9 remediation record. Do not mark the track complete from this page alone.
